## Plain text for pasting (one paragraph per beat)

Orders don't arrive in neat forms.

A WhatsApp message. A voice note. A handwritten list, photographed on a phone. An email.

Every one a different shape. Every one typed in by hand.

Meet MAIA. Forward any of them, right where your team already works. WhatsApp.

MAIA reads the message. Listens to the voice note. Reads the handwriting.

It matches every item to the right SKU, the right unit, and the price you agreed with that customer. A messy order becomes a clean, structured one.

Then MAIA prepares the quotation.

The sales order.

The delivery order.

And the invoice. Same order. Same details. Nothing retyped. Your team reviews, corrects and confirms.

Everything syncs to your ERP, SQL Account or AutoCount, so your books stay in step.

MAIA. Lives in WhatsApp. Syncs to your ERP. Start at maia dot wasap dot my.
